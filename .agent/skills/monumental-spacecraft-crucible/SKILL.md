## 0. The 5 Immutable Axioms of Monumental Sci-Fi Spacecraft

### Axiom 1: Monumental Scale & Architectural Hierarchy (Quy Mô Đại Thể)
- The vessel is not a fighter plane or a toy satellite. It is an exploration flagship (*Interstellar* Endurance, *2001* Discovery One, Tsutomu Nihei *Sidonia*, Syd Mead industrial realism).
- Must possess a clear 5-stage anatomical hierarchy:
  1. **Forward Command & Sensor Array**: Heavy hexagonal forward sensor prow, communications dish, recessed observation ports.
  2. **Rotating Habitat Centrifuge**: Continuous circular habitat ring with counter-balancing struts, slowly rotating around the central axis for artificial gravity.
  3. **Central Structural Spine / Keel**: Exposed carbon-titanium truss lattices, pressurized cargo canisters, and utility fuel conduits.
  4. **Planar Heat Radiator Wings**: Giant accordion-folded or monolithic thermal radiators extending laterally (the hallmark of real nuclear/fusion spacecraft).
  5. **Magnetic Confinement Fusion Drive**: Giant flared magnetic exhaust nozzle, magnetic coil rings, and collimated relativistic plasma plume.

### Axiom 2: Anti-Plasticity & Micro-Greebling (Tuyệt Đối Không "Nhựa")
- Zero smooth, primitive geometry without structural purpose.
- Every hull plate must have bevels, panel seams, structural ribbing, or recessed maintenance bays.
- Use faceted normals, high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting, and screen-space depth to give the metal weight, density, and tangible physical presence.

### Axiom 3: Pure Swiss Brutalist Chiaroscuro (Đen Trắng Thuần Khiết)
- **$100\%$ Monochrome Palette**: Matte graphite carbon (`#0a0a0f`), brushed aerospace titanium (`#c8c8d0`), pure specular highlights (`#ffffff`).
- **Zero Color Contamination**: No blue neon stripes, no red accents, no arcade lights.
- Illumination comes purely from the solar irradiance of Sol (directional sun) and the blinding incandescent white of the fusion drive.

### Axiom 4: Audio-Reactive Plasma Dynamics (Động Cơ Phản Ứng Âm Nhạc)
- The collimated fusion plume must react organically to the audio engine's sub-bass energy:
  - Base thrust: Stable, razor-sharp needle-like relativistic exhaust jet with shock diamonds.
  - Sub-bass kick: Deep thermal pulse expansion and forward coil luminance flare, without camera-shaking jitter.

### Axiom 5: Zero GC & 120 FPS Performance
- Procedural geometry constructed with pre-allocated buffer geometries and instanced meshes where applicable.
- In-frame transforms (centrifuge rotation, engine pulse) update via direct matrix/uniform manipulation with 0 array allocations per frame.

## 1. The 10-Point Monumental Vessel Scoring Rubric

Each iterative cycle must be photographed with headless Puppeteer and graded across 5 core dimensions (0 to 2 points each, maximum 10 points):

| Dimension | 0 Points (Deficient) | 1 Point (Acceptable) | 2 Points (Flawless 10/10) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Scale & Silhouette** | Clumsy toy-like proportions; feels small, flat, or indistinct. | Recognizable ship shape, but lacks imposing kilometer-class presence. | **Awe-inspiring architectural silhouette**; commanding spine, grand proportions, stark shadow profile against Sol. |
| **2. Micro-Greebling & Realism** | Smooth plastic cylinders; primitive boxes glued together. | Some surface detail, but still looks CG/gamey. | **Intricate industrial greebling**; structural trusses, pressurized modules, radiator ribs, zero plastic artifacts. |
| **3. Kinetic Centrifuge Dynamics** | Static or rigid; no rotating components; lifeless. | Rotates but stutters or lacks mechanical anchoring. | **Hypnotic, majestic mechanical rotation**; counter-weighted struts, smooth Keplerian angular velocity. |
| **4. Fusion Propulsion & Lighting** | No engine exhaust, or flat static sprite cone. | Visible plume, but lacks intensity and optical falloff. | **Blinding collimated relativistic ion/fusion plume**; magnetic pinch rings, organic sub-bass pulse, pure white. |
| **5. Composition & Cinematic Integration** | Vessel obscures the system awkwardly or clips out of view. | Well-placed, but camera angle feels ordinary. | **Interstellar-tier vista framing**; ship leads the viewer's eye into the solar system, Saturn, and Sol. |
